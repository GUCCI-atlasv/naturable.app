About

Writing should sound like a person

AI writing tools are everywhere, and they all leave the same fingerprints — the same words, the same rhythm, the same tidy little conclusions. Naturable exists to help you find those fingerprints and wipe them off, so your writing reads like you wrote it.

We took a different path from the usual "humanizer." Instead of a black box that promises to beat a detector, Naturable is transparent: it names each AI tell, explains why it reads as machine-written, and shows you the fix. The core checker is free and runs entirely in your browser, because good editing shouldn't cost anything or cost you your privacy.

What we believe

  • Clarity over tricks. We help you write better, not game a system.
  • Privacy by default. Free checks never leave your device.
  • Honest about limits. No tool can guarantee anything against a specific detector, and we won't pretend otherwise.
